**"LabVIEW Virtual Instruments Project Development & Practice"**
- **Author**: Gaoke Yang
- **ISBN**: 978-7-302-603238
- **Published by**:Tsinghua University Press, Jul 15, 2022.

This book is the 2nd revision of <a href="/">**"LabVIEW Virtual Instruments Project Developing & Management"**</a>, mainly about the virtual instruments project developing and management technologies based on LabVIEW platform. It can be used as reference material for virtual instrument developers, also can be used as textbook or training material for university/college students.There are plenty of source code examples, templates and frameworks for virtual instruments applications to support your innovation. Online video courses are also available to shorter your learning curve.
